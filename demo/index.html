TinyDoom

Two small neural networks predict what Doom will show next from the current frame and the player's action, and each has to build on its own predictions until it is shown a real frame again.

      What this demo does not show

      • The frames are 64 by 64 pixels, from one map (ViZDoom "My Way Home"), with three actions: forward, turn left, and turn right.
      • The episodes here are test episodes that the models were not trained on, but they come from the same map.
      • Compare mode uses recorded actions. Play mode lets you hold a key to move, but only from a fixed real start frame, with no new real frames.
      • A blurry picture can still have a low error, so judge the pictures as well as the chart.
      • The chart covers only the few episodes played on this page. The table gives the scores on the whole test split.
